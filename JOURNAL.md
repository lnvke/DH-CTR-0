# 3/24/2026 12 AM - Finished Base Schematic

_Time spent: 4.5h_

Following the guide, I finished the schematic for the RP2040 devboard. I was confused about the various resistances used for pullup resistors, and took some time researching the specific use for them. Likewise, I was also confused about decoupling capacitors in the same way, and so I spent some time researching the purpose and how the value affects the performance of the signal.

A small side-tangent I took during my research was on the various protocols used by electronic components to communicate with each other, such as serial TX/RX, I2C, and SPI; I also researched display protocols such as HDMI and the various types of MIPI display serial interfaces.

Additionally, I also spent a good amount of time in the RP2040 datasheet to fully understand its clock rate and its voltage ratings to finalize the decision on the crystal oscillator. Most notably, the 15 picofarad capacitors on the oscillator circuit made me wonder about the oscillation rate and its delay, because after I learned about debounce capacitors I began to apply my knowledge here. It turns out that the capacitors are placed with special capacitance to ensure a perfect high and low signal, as they provide a delay such that the crystal oscillates itself exactly 180 degrees out of phase.

From here, I neeed to decide the specific parts that I would use, the crystal oscillator being one of them. Likewise, I would also have to find my own USB-C receptacle that fit the footprint required.
